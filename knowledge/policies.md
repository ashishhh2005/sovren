# Acme Corp — Internal IT & HR Policy (sample knowledge base)

## Leave
- Full-time employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Leave must be requested at least 3 working days in advance.
- Unused leave carries over up to a maximum of 6 days into the next year.

## Work from home
- Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
- WFH days must be logged in the HR portal before 10:00 AM that day.
- Client-facing roles require manager approval for any WFH day.

## Equipment
- New laptops are issued within 5 working days of a raised IT ticket.
- Damaged equipment is replaced free of charge if the damage is accidental.
- Lost equipment is charged at 50% of its book value to the employee.

## Reimbursements
- Travel reimbursements must be filed within 30 days of the trip.
- Any single expense above 5000 INR requires manager pre-approval.
- Reimbursements are paid out with the next payroll cycle after approval.

## IT support
- Password resets are self-service via the IT portal.
- For account lockouts, raise a ticket; critical lockouts are resolved within 2 hours.
- Software installation requests need manager sign-off for paid licenses.
